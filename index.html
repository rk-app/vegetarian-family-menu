How to use this month

Shop from one weekly list at a time. Soft, small pieces for the toddler; same meals for adults. Offer milk (1% or 2%) or fortified soy with meals; water between meals. General meal planning only — not medical advice.

Weekly menus & grocery lists

Always-on pantry

Eggs, milk, yogurt, cheese, canned beans/lentils, tofu, oats, rice, pasta, whole-grain bread, peanut butter, olive oil, canned tomatoes, iron-fortified cereal, spices (cumin, turmeric, mild chili, cinnamon), frozen berries/peas/spinach.

Ontario year-round: eggs, dairy, carrots, apples, potatoes. Fall focus: squash, kale, cabbage, pears, broccoli.

Toddler notes

Texture & safety. Soft pieces; no whole nuts; thin nut butters; halve grapes. Mix some white rice/pasta with whole grain so fibre does not fill a small stomach too fast.

Milk. Ages 2+: 1% or 2% at meals. Limit juice. Ask your provider about vitamin D.

Busy-night swap

Any night: eggs + vegetables + grain (toast, rice, or roti). Still a complete vegetarian meal. Freeze doubles of chili, dal, and lasagna in Weeks 1–3 for Week 4.

Sources: Canada’s Food Guide; Ontario Dietitians in Public Health menu patterns; Foodland Ontario seasonal guide; HealthLink BC vegetarian toddler feeding.
